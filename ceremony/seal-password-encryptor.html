🔐 Spellbook Fresh Install — Seal Ceremony

Three steps. You and the agent pass encrypted blobs back and forth in chat. Nothing secret ever travels in cleartext.

⚠️ DO NOT RELOAD OR CLOSE THIS PAGE until the ceremony is done.
The reveal private key lives only in this page's memory. Reloading destroys it and you'll have to start over.
STEP 1

Give the agent your reveal key

👤 You do
  1. Click Generate reveal key below.
  2. Click Copy public key.
  3. Paste it into chat for the agent.
🤖 Agent does Validates your key is RSA-2048, then encrypts your full recovery document (both 24-word sets, all private keys and addresses) to it. The agent sends the encrypted envelope back as a copy-paste block in chat.
STEP 2

Decrypt your recovery backup

👤 You do
  1. Paste the envelope the agent sent into the box below.
  2. Click Decrypt backup.
  3. Verify: both 24-word sets are present, and the SET 1 EVM address matches what the agent told you.
  4. Write everything on paper, offline. Both mnemonics, all private keys, all addresses.
  5. Tell the agent: "backup recorded". Do not proceed until it's on paper.
🤖 Agent does Waits. It will not swap keys, seal, or delete anything until you confirm the paper backup is safely recorded.
STEP 3

Encrypt your new seal password

👤 You do
  1. Type a brand-new seal password (min 12 characters) — or click Generate for 8 random words.
  2. Write it on paper FIRST. This password seals your keys; losing it locks you out.
  3. Click Encrypt password, then Copy ciphertext.
  4. Paste the ciphertext into chat for the agent.
🤖 Agent does
  1. Validates the ciphertext and confirms its private key matches this page.
  2. Stops the daemon, atomically swaps in your fresh keys.
  3. Decrypts your password in memory only (never printed, logged, or stored) and pipes it straight to spellbook-seed seal.
  4. Restarts the daemon, verifies it derives your fresh addresses.
  5. Shreds every temporary plaintext file.
Fully offline. No network calls. Keys never leave this page except encrypted.
