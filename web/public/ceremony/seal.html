Spellbook seal password

Seal your wallet's seed with a password, or unlock it after the agent's machine was wiped. The password is encrypted here, in your browser, to your daemon's public key. Your agent only carries the encrypted envelope; it cannot read it.

Never type this password into chat. This page makes no network requests (its Content-Security-Policy forbids them). Check the page against the published hashes at /onboard/integrity if you want to be sure it is the reviewed code.

Give the envelope to your agent
